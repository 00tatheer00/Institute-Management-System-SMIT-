# Mohsin and Huma IT Center × SMIT Portal
## Student Portal User Guide

Welcome to the **Mohsin and Huma IT Center × SMIT Portal**! This guide assists students in accessing lectures, tracking attendance, submitting assignments, taking assessments, and receiving course certificates.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Student Account
- **URL**: `https://mhit.edu.pk/login`
- Enter your registration email and password.
- Upon login, you will be taken to your **Student Dashboard** (`/student`).

---

## 2. Tracking Your Academic Progress

### A. Attendance Records
1. Go to **Attendance** (`/student/attendance`).
2. Review your cumulative attendance percentage.
3. *Important*: Institute policy mandates a minimum **75% attendance rate** to qualify for graduation and certificate issuance.

### B. Assignments & Submissions
1. Go to **Assignments** (`/student/assignments`).
2. Review open assignments, required rubrics, and deadlines.
3. Click on an assignment to submit your GitHub repository link, live preview URL, or archive file.
4. Once graded by your trainer, inspect your score and constructive feedback.

### C. Online Quizzes
1. Go to **Quizzes** (`/student/quizzes`).
2. Timed quizzes appear when scheduled by your trainer.
3. Complete questions within the allotted duration and submit. Results and answer explanations appear upon completion.

### D. Academic Transcripts & GPA
1. Go to **Results** (`/student/results`).
2. Review your grades across all completed terms and view GPA calculations.

---

## 3. Graduation Certificates & Portfolio
1. **Certificates** (`/student/certificates`): View and download your official digital certificate. Each credential includes a unique QR code and cryptographic verification serial.
2. **Projects** (`/student/projects`): Showcase your capstone web, mobile, or AI projects on the public institute directory to potential employers.
3. **Documents** (`/student/documents`): Download official enrollment letters, fee waivers, and academic bonafide certificates.

---

## 4. Support & Communication Preferences
1. **Support Tickets** (`/student/support`): Submit inquiries or technical issues to administration and track responses.
2. **Notifications** (`/student/notifications`): Review all campus alerts, deadlines, and grades.
3. **Settings** (`/student/settings`): Toggle language preferences between **English** and **Urdu (اردو)**, and customize your WhatsApp and Email notification preferences.
