# Mohsin and Huma IT Center × SMIT Portal
## Trainer Operational Handover Guide

Welcome to the **Mohsin and Huma IT Center × SMIT Portal**. This manual guides instructors and teaching assistants through managing assigned batches, recording attendance, evaluating student work, and delivering curriculum materials.

---

## 1. Trainer Login & Dashboard
- **URL**: `https://mhit.edu.pk/login`
- Enter your assigned trainer email and password.
- Upon login, you are redirected to `/trainer`, where you will see your active batches, upcoming lecture schedule, and pending assignment submissions.

---

## 2. Daily Attendance Marking
1. Navigate to **Attendance** (`/trainer/attendance`).
2. Select your batch and class date.
3. The roster of enrolled students appears:
   - Click **Mark All Present** for quick entry.
   - Toggle individual students to **Absent**, **Late**, or **Excused**.
   - Add optional remarks (e.g. "Medical certificate submitted").
4. Click **Save Attendance**.
5. *Note*: Marking a student absent automatically triggers an instant absent notification to the student, keeping records transparent.

---

## 3. Assignment Management & Grading
1. Navigate to **Assignments** (`/trainer/assignments`).
2. Click **Create Assignment** to specify title, batch, deadline, total marks, and description.
3. When students submit work, navigate to the **Submissions** tab.
4. Review submitted GitHub repositories, live links, or uploaded code files.
5. Enter obtained marks and construct feedback.
6. Click **Submit Evaluation**. The gradebook updates automatically, and the student receives a notification with their score.

---

## 4. Quizzes & Assessments
1. Navigate to **Quizzes** (`/trainer/quizzes`).
2. Click **Create Quiz** or edit an existing one using the quiz builder.
3. Add multiple-choice questions, set time limits, passing percentage, and scheduled start time.
4. Once completed, inspect class analytics and individual question difficulty breakdowns.

---

## 5. Course Materials & Announcements
1. **Materials** (`/trainer/materials`): Upload lecture slides, PDF guides, code templates, and cheat sheets. Students enrolled in the batch receive immediate access.
2. **Announcements** (`/trainer/announcements`): Broadcast classroom reminders, schedule adjustments, or lab guidelines directly to your cohort.
